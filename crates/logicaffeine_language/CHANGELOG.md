## [0.11.0] - 2026-11-02

### Added

- **`with sigmoid gate Z`.** `gated delta of …` takes `with sigmoid gate Z` beside `with gate Z`: the normed head
  gated by `σ(z)`, as Qwen3.8-Flash-Next's Gated DeltaNet gates it, where `with gate Z` gates by `silu(z)`
  (`tensor_gated_delta_gate_lock`).
- **`capped at C`.** After an attention's scale, `capped at C` takes every score `s` as `C·tanh(s/C)` before the softmax
  (Gemma 2's soft cap), and `Let Y be X capped at C` caps every element of a tensor, both written as
  `C·(1 − 2/(e^(2x/C) + 1))`, which reaches `±C` in both tails. A cap that is not a positive number written out is
  refused by name (`tensor_softcap_lock`).
- **`within chunks of C`.** The attention sugars take a chunked-local window after their attended length: a query
  attending `n` positions keeps those of its own chunk, `⌊(n − 1)/C⌋·C + 1 ..= n` (Llama 4's rotated layers).
  `C` must be a positive whole number (`tensor_attention_window_lock`).
- **`moe … by sigmoid` and `weighting the inputs`.** After `choosing K of E`, `by sigmoid` scores each expert by its
  own logit's sigmoid in place of the softmax over every expert, and after the renormalizing clause `weighting the
  inputs` scales each kept expert's input by its weight and adds its output unweighted (Llama 4's routing). A malformed
  clause is refused by name (`tensor_moe_routing_lock`).
- **DeepSeek-V3's routing in `moe`.** After the scoring, `biased by B` chooses each expert by its score plus `B[e]`
  and weighs it by its score; `within the top G of N groups` scores each of `N` groups of experts by its two best
  selection scores and chooses only among the top `G` groups'; after the renormalizing clause, `scaled by S`
  multiplies every kept weight by `S`. A clause out of its order, a group limit that does not divide the experts or
  keeps fewer than are chosen, and a scale that is not a positive number are refused by name
  (`tensor_moe_grouped_routing_lock`).
- A reactive signal's value family is settled at its DECLARATION, and `Real` is one of the families. The `a signal <init>` desugar consults the binding's declared type (`Let rate: Real be a signal 2`), the initial value's shape, and — scanning the remaining tokens — whether a later statement writes a fractional number into it; a whole-number start for a `Real` signal is carried as a real, and integral writes to one store reals (`Set rate to 3` stores `3.0`, `Increase rate by 1` adds `1.0`). Reads and writes route through the `signalNewFloat`/`signalGetFloat`/`signalSetFloat` family, exactly as `Text` and `Seq` signals already did, which is what lets a view and the event handler that writes it agree about the same cell.
- The action vocabulary as first-class statements: `Stmt::Ui` (element verbs `Focus` / `Scroll to` / `Add class … to` / `Remove class … from` / `Select the text in` / `Copy … to the clipboard` / `Download … as`, navigation `Navigate to` / `Replace the URL with` / `Go back` / `Set the title to`, persistence `Save … as` / `Load … into` / `Forget`, feedback `Notify`, state `Toggle`, `Call <Store>'s <action>`, `Stop the timer`, and `Mount <Component> [at …]`) and `Stmt::Fetch` (`Get` / `Post` / `Put` / `Patch` / `Delete` / `Upload` … `as <Type>, then <action>`). They are component vocabulary: offered only inside a component definition, so an existing program's sentences keep their meaning.
- The LGX component model as first-class AST: `ast::component` (`ComponentDef` for `## Page` / `## Component` / `## Store` / `## Error page`, with its `State:` fields — typed, inferred, or `always be` derived — its `Actions:` procedures and the English `Trigger` vocabulary they answer, its `View:` tree and `Style:` block) plus `Stmt::{ComponentDef, DirectiveDef, RouteDef, MiddlewareDef, RedirectDef}` and `Expr::View`. `parser/component.rs` parses all seven headers and hands every expression a template holds — a hole, a condition, an iterable, a directive value — to the ordinary LOGOS expression parser at its own byte offset, so an error inside a hole is reported at the hole and not at the section header.
- `lgx` — the LOGOS web framework's template layer: `lgx::check` (structural errors — content outside `{block}`s in an extending view, duplicate blocks or `{children}`, `bind` on a non-control, `class:` on a component — and an accessibility lint for images without `alt`, buttons with no name, unlabelled form controls and links without `href`, each finding with its span and the fix), `lgx::template::print` (the deterministic, idempotent inverse of `parse`, for round-trips and `largo expand`), `lgx::html` (a flat HTML tokenizer with string-aware `{…}` groups, entities, void elements and the `<script>`/`<style>`/doctype refusals), `lgx::template` (the Jinja-style tree: `{if}`/`{else if}`/`{else}`, `{for each … keyed by … at index …}` with an empty arm, `{inspect}`/`{when}`, `{let}`, `{extends}`/`{block}`, `{children}`, `{raw}`, `{# #}`, holes with `|` filters, component tags, `on-<event>.<mods>`/`bind`/`class:` directives, whitespace rules, thirteen Socratic error kinds; every LOGOS expression is kept as text plus byte offset for the real expression parser), and `lgx::css` (component scoping by a real tokenizer: `.card h1` → `.card h1.lgx-todos`, `:global(…)`, group at-rules recurse, other at-rules verbatim). The lexer captures `View:` / `Style:` section bodies and `## To … -> View:` bodies verbatim as `TokenType::RawBlock { kind, body, content_start }`, and reads the new headers `## Page /path`, `## Route METHOD /path`, `## Store`, `## Directive`, `## Before each request`, `## Redirect /a -> /b.`, `## Error page 404` by the shape of their line (prose headings that share a first word stay prose).
- `ast::view` — the view IR (`ViewDef`, `ViewNode`, `Attr`, `ViewRoot`, with a deterministic `shape()` printer): `Render:` / `Render into` / a component's `View:` now parse into this tree first (`parser/view.rs`), and one lowering walk (`parser/view_lower.rs`) emits the statements the execution tiers run. `Parser::views()` / `take_views()` expose the trees in source order for tooling.
- `token_class::imperative_keywords!` / `is_imperative_keyword`: every statement word of the language, in one list the LSP's semantic tokens and the Studio's keyword colouring both read.
- `symbol_dict::notation()`: every symbol LOGOS writes with a fixed spelling, as the Unicode view prints it, with what it means (the quantifiers, connectives and modals, identity, and the deontic O). The Learn page's symbol buttons take their meanings from it.

- `Let dx be an infinitesimal much smaller than dt.`, `much larger than`, and both bounds joined by `and` (`parser::nonstandard`), lowered to `orderedInfinitesimal(name, larger, smaller, modulus, residues…)`; a bare `smaller than` or `larger than` is refused naming what it leaves undecided. `relative to <infinitesimal>` after the standard part and the `is infinitesimal`, `is limited` and `is infinitely close to` predicates passes the standpoint as their last argument.
- `the real number whose digits in base k are f` and `the real number approximated by f` (`parser::nonstandard`): the builtin `definedReal(kind, base, name, (n: Int) -> f(n))`. A base below two, or one not written out, is refused where it is written.
- The integer tensor forms: `X times M shifted right by S`, `X clamped between LO and HI`, `relu of X` and `lut
  attention of Q over K and V with H heads KV groups up to P with table E and scale M shifted right by S`, each
  desugared to the loops every tier runs.
- `Opt::ModWrap` runs on the VM path as well as AOT and codegen.
- **`within a window of W`.** `attention … up to P`, `causal attention … from P` and the unbounded `attention … groups` take a window after their attended length: a query attending `n` positions keeps `max(1, n − W + 1) ..= n`, bound once per head, and the score, softmax and weighted-sum loops run over those positions alone. `W` must be a positive whole number (`tensor_attention_window_lock`).
- **`with sinks S`.** An attention's last clause gives each query head a logit that joins its softmax's maximum and denominator and takes no value; `S` must be one row of a logit per head (`tensor_attention_sinks_lock`).
- **A row over a block.** `A plus B` and `A hadamard B` take a `B` of one row as wide as `A`'s rows and combine it with each of them, read at each element's column (`tensor_row_broadcast_lock`).
- **A mixture's biases and clamped gate.** `moe of X with router Wr plus Br gate Wg plus Bg up Wu plus Bu down Wd plus Bd choosing K of E` adds each bias where its weight's outputs are, and `using a swiglu clamped at L with slope A` gates with `min(g, L)·σ(A·min(g, L))·(clamp(u, −L, L) + 1)` (`tensor_moe_options_lock`).
- **`X scaled by C`.** A tensor times a number written out, element by element, into a tensor of `Real`s of `X`'s shape (`tensor_scale_lock`).
- **A norm names its epsilon.** `rmsnorm`, `layernorm`, `l2norm` and `gated delta` take `with epsilon E` after their operands, `E` a positive number, and desugar to a norm that divides by `sqrt(… + E)`; without the clause the epsilon is 1e-6. Anything but a positive number after `with epsilon` is refused, naming the norm (`tensor_norm_epsilon_lock`).
- **Heads taken apart and put together.** `the first K of every N of X` and `the last K of every N of X` keep one part of every group of `N` consecutive elements; `A joined with B` puts two tensors' rows side by side, and `across H heads` each head's parts; `X matmul W across H heads` multiplies each head's slice of `X`'s rows by its own block of `W`'s rows. Each desugars to counted loops whose bounds name every dimension. A part past its group, columns that are not whole groups, a join of different rows or of widths that do not divide among the heads, and a `W` without a row for each column of `X` are refused by name (`tensor_latent_attention_lock`).
- **An attention's values and its scale.** An attention reads values of their own width (`V` with `K`'s rows and `KV·VD` columns), and `scaled by S` after its window replaces the score scale `1/√HD`. With the head forms they write DeepSeek's latent attention with each head's query absorbed into the cached latent (`tensor_latent_attention_lock`).
- **`without renormalizing`.** `moe … choosing K of E without renormalizing` weighs each kept expert by its softmax over every expert, DeepSeek-V2's routing; anything but `renormalizing` after `without` is refused (`tensor_moe_weighting_lock`).
- **`Opt::ModWrap` (`modwrap`).** A remainder by a constant `m > 0` of a value the range oracle bounds to `[0, 2m)` compiles to one conditional subtraction, and a value below `m` to itself, on AOT-Rust and the C backend; a power-of-two `m` takes `strengthreducepow2`'s mask instead, which preempts it. `mod_wrap_lock`.
- **`Opt::BitPack` (`bitpack`).** A local `Seq of Bool` used only through item reads, item stores, pushes and its length is held one bit per item on AOT-Rust. `bit_pack_lock`.
- **`Opt::VariantBox` (`variantbox`).** A variant of a recursive enum that holds the enum keeps its fields in one box on AOT-Rust, where each such field had a box of its own; a fieldless variant allocates nothing. `variant_box_lock`.
- **`Opt::BranchlessSelect` (`branchlessselect`).** `If c: Set x to a. Otherwise: Set x to b.`, both arms integer arithmetic that cannot trap and at least one exact behind a guarded dual, computes both arms and selects with `c` on AOT-Rust; each arm's guard is tested on its own, and the original branch runs when one fails. `branchless_select_lock`.
- **`Opt::MaskNarrow` (`masknarrow`).** An integer chain of `+`, `-`, `*`, `&`, `|`, `^` and constant `<<` under `& (2^k - 1)`, or `% 2^k` of a non-negative value, with `k <= 32`, whose every `+`/`-`/`*` the range oracle keeps within i64, computes in 32-bit wrapping arithmetic on AOT-Rust and both C backends: the low `k` bits are a function of the operands' low bits alone. On the `% 2^k` it computes it preempts `strengthreducepow2`. `mask_narrow_lock`.

- **Tests are written in the language.** `## Test: <prose>` declares a test named by its sentence (a duplicate name in one module is a parse error); its body is ordinary imperative LOGOS plus `Expect that <condition>.` (every failed expectation is reported, not only the first), `Expect this to fail [with "<message>"]:`, `Expect output:` with an indented snapshot or `Expect output as in "<file>".`, `Advance time by <duration>.` and `Given a file "<path>" containing "<text>".`. Leading sentences set `Skip this test because "<reason>".` (the reason is required), `This test may use the real filesystem.` (or `network`, `environment`, `clock`) and `This test may run up to N steps.`. `For each of these cases:` followed by a Markdown pipe table binds the table's columns and runs one named sub-test per row; `For all integers a and b [where <condition>]:` (or `naturals`, `booleans`) is a seeded property that shrinks a counterexample. A test-only statement outside a `## Test` or `## Example` is refused with a lesson. A failed `Expect` renders its condition back to English with the value of each named part beside it (`printer::{render_expr, subexpressions}`). New AST: `Stmt::{TestDef, Expect, ExpectFail, ExpectOutput, AdvanceTime, GivenFile, ForAll}`, `ast::{TestCapabilities, CaseTable, ExpectedOutput, PropDomain}`, `BlockType::Test`. `test_runner_lock`, `property_test_lock`, `test_surface_catalog`, `failure_message_goldens`.
- **Observation statements.** `Note that …`, `Warn that …` and `Record that …` emit developer, operator and audit events (`ast::ObsCategory`), with payloads (`with <x> and <y>`) and deterministic per-site sampling (`Note once in every 100 times that …`); `During <gerund>:` opens a lexical trace span. `Stmt::{Observe, During}`; `during` is a statement word only in imperative code, and prose keeps the preposition. An observation never changes program output on any tier, and an elided one compiles to nothing. `obs_grammar_catalog`, `obs_emission_lock`, `obs_tier_differential`.
- **Programs span modules: the `Import` grammar.** v0.10.1 read a module's dependencies only from Markdown links in its opening paragraph. `Import Geometry.`, `Import Geometry and Colors.` / `Import Lexer, Parser, Ast.`, `Import Geometry from "shapes/geometry.lg".`, `Import Geometry as Geo.` and the selective `Import double from Util.` / `Import double as dbl from Util.` (`analysis::SelectedName`, `Dependency::selection`) count anywhere in a module's prose above its first `##` header; an indented or fenced line is an illustration, never an import, and `analysis::{first_section_header, misplaced_imports}` find the imports written below the header for the compiler to refuse. `Geometry::Point` lexes as one qualified name, `private` keeps a definition inside its module, and `TypeRegistry::{namespaced_twins, canonical_type}` make a bare type name and its qualified twin one type. `import_format::normalize_imports` sorts and de-duplicates the import block for `largo fmt`. `import_scan_lock`, `project_module_lock`, `multimodule_selective`, `multimodule_encapsulation`, `imported_record_fetch_lock`.
- **Contracts carry a reason, a program can fail on purpose, and range contracts take units.** `Fail with <reason>.` (`Stmt::Fail`) raises; with no `Result` to return into and no `Try:` around it, the program stops with the reason. `Assert that`, `Require that` and the new input contract `Assume [that] <condition>.` accept `, otherwise <reason>`, evaluated only when the contract breaks (`Stmt::RuntimeAssert.reason`); a reason that is not Text is refused in one sentence on every tier. `Assume v is between 0.2 volts and 0.7 volts.` (or `between 200 and 700 millivolts`: a unit on one bound distributes) folds each literal bound to its exact SI magnitude at parse time and records the dimension on `RuntimeAssert.dim`; bounds in two dimensions, or a dimensioned bound that is not a literal, are refused where written. `error_fail_and_contracts`, `analog_dimensioned_contract_lock`.
- **Tensor sugar.** `Let A be a R by C tensor of Int.` (or `Real`) declares a shaped, row-major tensor, and `Let … be` accepts `A matmul B` (shape-checked), `A plus B`, `A hadamard B`, `softmax of X` (per row), `rmsnorm of X [plus R] with weight W [across H heads]`, `layernorm of X with weight W and bias B`, `l2norm of X [across H heads]`, `silu`, `sigmoid`, `gelu` (tanh form), `swiglu of G and U`, `rope of Q with cos C and sin S [from position P] [across H heads]`, `decay of …`, `causal conv of X with weight W state S over T taps`, `attention of Q over K and V with H heads G groups [up to P]`, `causal attention … from P`, `full attention of Q over K and V with H heads`, `gated delta of …`, `moe of X with router Wr gate Wg up Wu down Wd choosing K of E`, `argmax of X`, `transpose of X`, `row i of T` and `rows I of T`, with `Set row i of T to V.` and `Set rows from P of T to X.`. Each form desugars in the parser to ordinary sequences and loops, so every tier runs it and the accelerator backends recognize the idioms; shapes are checked at parse time and row positions at run time, with one message on every tier. `Parser::tensor_shapes()`. `tensor_sugar_tier_lock`, `tensor_op_coverage_lock`, `tensor_sugar_all_backends_lock`.

### Breaking changes
- **`OptimizationConfig` and `FiredOptimizations` hold an `OptBits` set.** The registry outgrew one `u64` (`Tile` was its 64th bit): `OptBits` is `[u64; OPT_WORDS]` with `OPT_WORDS = OPT_COUNT.div_ceil(64)`, and `OptimizationConfig::bits`, `FiredOptimizations::{from_bits, bits}` take and return it. `Opt::bit` is gone. Migration: `config.bits().contains(opt)` for `config.bits() & opt.bit() != 0`, `OptBits::EMPTY.with(opt)` for `opt.bit()`.
- **Seven more `##` header words, and prose headings near them are refused.** `test`, `component`, `page`, `route`, `store`, `directive` and `redirect` joined the header words a heading is checked against. `## Test …` is always a test block and `## Component …` always a component; `## Page`, `## Route`, `## Store`, `## Directive` and `## Redirect` without their LGX shape (`## Page layout`, `## Store`, `## Route todos`) are parse errors; and a heading whose first word is within two edits (Levenshtein) of a header word is refused as a probable typo. v0.10.1 read all of these as prose. By that rule `## Tests`, `## Next steps`, `## Best practices`, `## Story`, `## State` and `## Usage` are now refused. `## Session` alone, `## Before each request` and `## Error page <status>` open code blocks; `## Error …` and `## Before …` headings without that shape stay prose. `lgx_headers_lock::a_typo_of_a_new_header_is_loud_not_prose`, `lgx_headers_lock::a_code_header_word_without_its_shape_is_loud`, `lgx_headers_lock::prose_headings_that_share_a_first_word_stay_prose`. Migration: as the refusal says, "if it is a prose heading, pick a name further from the code headers": start the heading with a word three or more edits from every header word (`## About the tests`, `## Notes on pages`).
- **A binding lent to a `mutable` parameter may not be used again in the same call.** `h(a, a)` and `h(a, popLast(a))`, where `h` takes `(xs: mutable Seq of Int)`, compiled in v0.10.1 and gave different answers on different tiers; they are refused where written, naming the binding, the parameter and the other use. `joined(a, popLast(a))`, which lends nothing to `joined`, is unchanged. `lend_exclusivity_lock`. Migration, as the refusal spells it: "Pass `copy of a` there, or take what you need from `a` into a `Let` before the call."
- **A declared function named like a statement word is called by that name.** A program that declares `## To show (x: Int):` (or `check`, `sleep`, `sync`, `listen`, `connect`, `spawn`, `receive`, `stop`, `trust`, `launch`, …) and writes `show(3).` now calls its function; v0.10.1 ran the statement form or refused the call. A statement word that names no declared function keeps its meaning. `declared_function_call_statement_lock`. Migration: rename the function, or write the statement form (`Show 3.`).
- **An interpolation hole must parse whole.** A hole whose tokens did not all parse rendered what did and dropped the rest (`"x{5 garbage here}y"` showed `x5y`; `"{"a":1}"` showed `a`), and a width on a text literal was accepted; both are refused. `interpolation_hole_total_lock`. Migration, as the refusal spells it: for literal braces (a JSON object, a CSS rule) "double them: `{{` and `}}` print `{` and `}`".
- **`r"…"` is verbatim.** A raw string kept its backslashes in v0.10.1 but still opened interpolation holes; it now has no holes, and `{{` stays doubled (`r"{x}"` shows `{x}`). Migration: write a string that needs a hole with plain quotes, escaping its backslashes.
- **The parse recursion budget is an eighth of the AST depth limit.** `ast_depth::max_parse_recursion()` is `max_ast_depth() / 8`, 16 levels at the default limit of 128; it was a quarter, 32, the depth at which the parser's descent exhausts a 2 MiB stack in a debug build, so a program at the old budget could overflow instead of being refused. Source nested past 16 levels, typically a machine-generated parenthesis tower, is `AstTooDeep`. `compiler_stack_safety_lock`. Migration, as the diagnostic spells it: bind each layer in its own `Let` ("Intermediate bindings reset the depth to one"), or raise `LOGOS_MAX_AST_DEPTH`.
- **An ambiguous or incomplete variant construction is refused.** A bare `a new Failure` whose variant two types declare resolved in hash order in v0.10.1; it is refused, teaching `a new Failure from Outcome`. An `Inspect` whose arms name variants of two enums is refused ("every arm of this Inspect must match variants of the SAME type"), and a variant construction that omits a field is refused (a struct field still defaults from its type). `variant_resolution_lock::two_user_enums_sharing_a_variant_name_refuse_a_bare_construction`, `constructor_field_boundary_lock`. Migration: write `a new <Variant> from <Enum>` to name the one you want; match one enum per `Inspect`; give every field a variant declares.
- **Theorem blocks are read as written.** A `Given:` below `Prove:` was left out of the premises; two premises citable by one name (`Given (h):` twice, or a label in the positional `hp<n>` namespace that is not its own index) were accepted. Both are refused. `language_L_language_extra`. Migration, as the refusals spell it: "Move every `Given:` line above the `Prove:` line"; give the premises different `Given (<label>):` names, and "choose a label outside the `hp<n>` namespace".
- **A rendered parse error names its kind by its headline.** `ParseError::display_with_source` wrote the kind's `Debug` form; it writes `ParseErrorKind::headline()`, and its did-you-mean hint appears only for an error about the word under the caret. Migration: tools that matched the error text match `ParseErrorKind::code()` (`logos-diagnostic/1`) instead.
- **The vocabulary file moved to `logicaffeine-lexicon`.** `logicaffeine-language` no longer carries `lexicon.json` in its own assets directory; its build script reads `logicaffeine_lexicon::LEXICON_JSON` through a build-dependency, and the file is `crates/logicaffeine_lexicon/assets/lexicon.json`, byte-identical. Migration: tools that read the file by path read `crates/logicaffeine_lexicon/assets/lexicon.json`, or use `logicaffeine_lexicon::LEXICON_JSON`.
- **Public enums gain variants and fields.** Exhaustive matches stop compiling on `Stmt` (27 new variants: `Swap`, `Fail`, `TestDef`, `Expect`, `ExpectFail`, `ExpectOutput`, `AdvanceTime`, `GivenFile`, `Observe`, `During`, `ForAll`, `Reversibly`, `JoinCrdt`, `Serve`, `Ui`, `Fetch`, `Replay`, `ServeFetches`, `GivenFetch`, `ComponentDef`, `DirectiveDef`, `RouteDef`, `MiddlewareDef`, `RedirectDef`, `CloseChannel`, `AwaitTask`, `Site`), `Expr` (`View`, `Dom`), `TypeExpr` (`Tuple`, `Record`), `TokenType` (`Swap`, `Expect`, `Advance`, `Skip`, `Note`, `Warn`, `Record`, `During`, `RawBlock`, `Converge`), `BlockType` (`Component`, `Session`, `Require`, `Test`, `Page`, `Store`, `Directive`, `Route`, `Middleware`, `Redirect`, `ErrorPage`), `CalendarUnit` (`Millisecond`, `Microsecond`, `Nanosecond`), `analysis::FieldType` (`Tuple`, `Record`) and `optimization::Opt` (23). `Stmt::RuntimeAssert` gains `dim` and `reason`, `Stmt::SendMessage` gains `unreliable`, and `analysis::Dependency` gains `selection`. The full set is committed as a golden in the LSP crate (`socratic_explanations.txt`) so every future wording change is a reviewable diff.
- The imperative statement dispatch diagnoses `x is 5.` as `IsValueEquality` (say `equals`) instead of a generic `ExpectedStatement` — the specific lesson was previously only reachable on the logic-mode path.
- **The formatter is now structure-aware and provably safe.** `format_source` reindents code to 4 spaces per LEXED nesting level (depth from the lexer's own Indent/Dedent reading — a double-tab first indent canonicalizes to 4 spaces, not 8), leaves `## Note`/`## Example` prose entirely to the author (markdown hard-breaks and nested-list indents survive), and — the bug fix — never touches a byte inside a multiline string (`format_line` was stripping trailing spaces, converting tabs, and blanking whitespace-only lines INSIDE `"""` literals: silent string corruption in both `largo fmt` and the LSP). Locked by `formatter_locks.rs`: token-stream equivalence (`tokens(format(x)) == tokens(x)`, string bytes included) + idempotence over the corpus.
- **The parser is total.** `Parser::new` EOF-terminates any fragment or block slice that arrives without one, and `peek()` clamps to the terminator instead of indexing out of bounds — a bare `## Theorem: Socrates` (header, no body) used to PANIC analysis, found by the new quickguide highlighting ratchet.
- The `UseAfterMove` socratic explanation now speaks the ownership house voice — "Cannot use 'x' after giving it away", with the transfer explained and both remedies named (`Show` to lend, `a copy of` to keep) — matching the ownership checker and the rustc diagnostic bridge instead of the mechanical "this value has been moved".

### Fixed
- 3sg stem recovery derived from the generative rule: `strip_s` accepts a stem only if it is a known base verb whose `third_person_of` regenerates the surface form exactly, so `planes` is no longer the 3sg of `plan`.
- Presupposition triggers read through `Ambiguous` tokens (Van der Sandt projection under negation); `strip_s` resolves sibilant `-es` and `-ies` stems via `is_base_verb`; a temporal-adverb reading is blocked right after a determiner; focus constructions accept copular predication (`Only dogs are red.`).
