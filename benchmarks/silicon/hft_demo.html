3 · Slow today, on purpose — and it scales straight

Measured: 0 jitter, on a $265 student board at 6.25 MHz, zero timing optimization. Slow in absolute terms — we won’t pretend otherwise. But our slowness is a clock number, not an architecture number:

latency  =  ~866 cycles  ÷  clock

The cycles are small and bounded (850–874). Two levers close the gap to nanoseconds — both already in our stack:

→ Faster clock.  Our Sky130 signoff (OpenLane, DRC/LVS-clean) closes 90 MHz today — ~103,926 trades/s; a modern ASIC is GHz.
→ Spatial fabric.  The same English also lowers to a dataflow pipeline — the strategy laid out in space, not fetched op-by-op — the real path to nanosecond latency at line rate.
